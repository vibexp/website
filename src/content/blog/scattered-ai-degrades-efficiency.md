AI is supposed to make you faster. So why does heavy AI use so often _feel_
slower than it should? The answer is rarely the model. It is everything around
the model — the small, repeated frictions that come from using powerful tools
without a system to organize them.

Scattered AI use does not announce itself. It hides in three habits that feel
harmless one at a time but compound across a day.

## 1. Lost prompts

You wrote the perfect prompt last week. This week you are rewriting it from
memory — slightly worse, and a few minutes slower. Multiply that by every
recurring task, and a meaningful share of your AI time goes to reconstructing
work you already did. A prompt you cannot find is a prompt you do not have.

## 2. Forgotten context

Every new chat starts from zero. You re-paste the project brief, re-state the
coding standards, re-explain the background the model needs to be useful. The
model has no memory of your work, so you become its memory — manually, on
repeat. That re-explaining tax is invisible precisely because it feels like
"just getting started."

## 3. Scattered outputs

The model produced something genuinely valuable: a snippet, a draft, an
analysis. Then the conversation moved on, and three days later it is somewhere
in an endless scroll-back you will not realistically search. Output you cannot
retrieve is output you did not really keep.

## Why it compounds

Each of these is minor in isolation — a minute here, a re-paste there. The
problem is frequency. Repeated across every session, every project, every day,
they turn into context-switching overhead and quiet rework that never shows up
on a to-do list but absolutely shows up in your hours.

## The fix is structure, not willpower

You do not solve scattered AI use by trying harder to remember. You solve it by
giving your AI assets a home:

- Save prompts as reusable templates instead of retyping them.
- Store project context once so it is ready in every session.
- Keep valuable outputs as searchable artifacts instead of losing them to
  chat history.
